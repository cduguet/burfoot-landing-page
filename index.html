— II —

One story becomes a world.
One character leads the journey.

Burfoot began on a page. A creature of the jungle with a coat like velcro — picking up every flower, feather and fern he brushed past — sets out to solve a simple question: why is the jungle so dark?

What he finds is not an answer but a world. And what the world finds in him is a way of being — curious, kind, unreasonably hopeful. The book is for ages 2 to 6. The wardrobe, for 0 to 14. The mission, for everyone who buys into either.

A world children can step into — anywhere.

Non-toxic by design

Every dye and finish passes ZDHC and OEKO-TEX thresholds. No PFAS, no phthalates, no formaldehyde.

Designed to circle back

When outgrown, send any garment back with prepaid post. We resell, re-patch, or recycle — and send your child a new patch of their choosing.

Pantone-current, mood-timeless

Each season we refresh palette — not shape. Garments stay in rotation for years. Patches let the wardrobe grow up with the child.
